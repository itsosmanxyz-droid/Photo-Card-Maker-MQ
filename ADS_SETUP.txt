ADMOB SETUP - Photo Card Maker MQ
=================================
AdMob app ID : ca-app-pub-9414513035654232~5010303925   (already in AndroidManifest.xml)
Ad units     : App open, Banner, Interstitial, Native, Rewarded, Rewarded interstitial
               (all written in src/com/osman/photocardmaker/ads/AdConfig.java)

1) ADD THE LIBRARIES (the only step you must do in your build tool)
   com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads:25.5.0
   com.google.android.ump:user-messaging-platform:4.0.0
   Requirements of that SDK version: minSdk 24+, compileSdk 35+ (minSdk is already 24 here).
   Gradle:   dependencies {
                 implementation 'com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads:25.5.0'
                 implementation 'com.google.android.ump:user-messaging-platform:4.0.0'
             }
   If your tool cannot use that version, copy ads_stub/AdsManager.java over
   src/com/osman/photocardmaker/ads/AdsManager.java and the app builds without ads.

2) TEST FIRST, THEN GO LIVE
   AdConfig.TEST_MODE = true  -> Google's test ads (safe, use this while developing).
   AdConfig.TEST_MODE = false -> your real ads. Set it ONLY for the build you publish.
   Never click your own live ads (AdMob policy, can suspend the account).

3) WHERE THE ADS APPEAR
   App open ........ returning to the app (after 3 launches, 4 min apart, not after picker/share)
   Banner .......... bottom of Template lists, My projects, Custom templates, Logo manager
   Native .......... home screen, between the two sections (marked "Ad")
   Interstitial .... after every 2nd export, when the "Saved" dialog is closed (90 s apart)
   Rewarded ........ Settings > "Watch a video: no ads for 1 hour"
   Rewarded inter. . Export at Full HD (3x): optional video, free HD (2x) always available
                     (turn off with AdConfig.GATE_FULL_HD = false)

4) IN YOUR ADMOB ACCOUNT
   - Privacy & messaging: create and publish a consent message (GDPR / US states) for this app.
   - Link the app to its store listing when published; real ads need a published app.
   - Add a privacy policy link to the store listing and fill the Data safety form
     (the ads SDK collects device and advertising data).

5) ADMOB POLICY REMINDERS
   - Do not click your own ads or ask users to click.
   - Use test ads while testing.
   - Keep ads away from buttons (no accidental clicks); native ads must be labelled "Ad".
   - Rewarded interstitial needs an intro screen with an option to skip (done in the editor).
   - Do not use the app for children under 13 without child-directed settings.
