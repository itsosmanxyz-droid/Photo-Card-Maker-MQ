Open THIS folder (the one with settings.gradle) as a NEW project in AIDE, with internet ON.
Runs on Android 10 (API 29) and newer only.
old_aide_build_output/ = your original bin/ and gen/ folders, untouched, not used by the build (safe to delete).
Switches: app/src/main/java/com/osman/photocardmaker/ads/AdIds.java (USE_TEST_ADS, TEST_DEVICE_IDS)
          app/src/main/java/com/osman/photocardmaker/ads/AdManager.java (GATE_HD_EXPORT)
Never click your own real ads.
